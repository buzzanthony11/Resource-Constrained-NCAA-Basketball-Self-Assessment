Program Strategic Archetype Assessment

A self-assessment for resource-constrained NCAA basketball programs (non-P4 Division I, Division II, NAIA, Division III). It identifies which of four strategic archetypes best fits how your program already operates, or would most naturally operate, in the NIL era.

Start with three quick questions about your situation, then 12 questions about strategy. For each strategy question, choose the statement that is most like your program, then the one that is second most like it. If your program has no NIL activity yet, answer for what would fit you best. About 5 minutes. There are no wrong answers: a strong fit is the goal, not a high score.

Draft instrument based on a thesis framework (Barney, 1991; Porter, 1996; Kim & Mauborgne, 2004; Zvosec & Bass, 2022). It is a decision aid, not a validated psychometric test.
